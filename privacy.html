Privacy Policy

Otomasyon Stüdyosu · Last updated: November 7, 2026

Otomasyon Stüdyosu ("the app") is a private desktop tool that runs only on its owner's own computer and publishes videos to YouTube channels managed by the owner. This policy explains what data the app accesses through Google and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app receives an OAuth access token and refresh token for that YouTube channel. With that permission it accesses only:

  • the channel's own basic information (channel ID, name) and public statistics (video, view and subscriber counts);
  • the channel's own uploaded videos and their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, publish schedule);
  • comments the app itself posts on the channel's own videos, and the channel's banner image.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, Google Drive, viewers' personal data, or any other Google service.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the app's single function: uploading, scheduling and managing the owner's own videos on the owner's own channels, and showing the owner a summary of channel statistics. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and channel statistics are stored only in local files on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the app. Access to these files is limited to the owner's computer account.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer data received from Google APIs to any third party. Data is sent only to Google (YouTube) itself, as needed to perform the actions described above.

Limited Use

Otomasyon Stüdyosu's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the channel remains connected to the app. A channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the stored tokens no longer work. When a channel is disconnected inside the app, the app revokes the token with Google and deletes the stored token file from the owner's computer. Deletion can also be requested by email (see below).

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, channel owners agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
