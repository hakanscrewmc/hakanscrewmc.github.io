Otomasyon Stüdyosu

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels

What the app does

Otomasyon Stüdyosu is a desktop application that runs only on its owner's own computer. The owner produces music and educational animation videos and uses this tool to publish them to YouTube channels that the owner personally manages. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

How it uses YouTube

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload finished videos to the authorized channel and schedule their publish date;
  • set the video thumbnail, title, description and tags, and change the scheduled publish date later if needed;
  • read the channel's own basic information and statistics (channel name, video list, view and subscriber counts) to plan uploads;
  • post a comment from the channel on its own videos and update the channel's own banner image.

The app only acts on the channels whose owner authorized it. It does not access other people's channels, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data.

Google sign-in scopes

  • youtube.upload — upload videos to the authorized channel
  • youtube.readonly — read the authorized channel's own information and statistics
  • youtube.force-ssl — update the authorized channel's own videos (metadata, schedule, thumbnail), comments and banner
